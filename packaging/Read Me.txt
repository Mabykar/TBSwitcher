TBSwitcher — Intel Macs, macOS 13 or newer

INSTALL
1. Open the included PKG and follow Installer. Enter your administrator password.
2. Open TBSwitcher from Applications.
3. Click the lightning icon in the menu bar to turn Turbo Boost off.
4. If macOS requests approval, open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   allow “Legacy Developer: Rugarciap”, and restart. Try the switch again.

This free community build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized.
If macOS blocks the installer, first attempt to open it, then go to
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway (“Все равно открыть”).
Only approve a download you trust. No global security settings need changing.
Apple Developer membership and developer tools are not required to install.

Left-click: toggle Turbo Boost. Right-click: settings and quit.
Apple Silicon is not supported. Preserve the original kext files unchanged.
The legacy Apple signature exception may not be available on every macOS version.

UNINSTALL
The included uninstall.command removes the app and its root helper and attempts
to unload the kext. Administrator access is required. Restart if necessary.
The kext is shared with the original Turbo Boost Switcher, so unloading it also
affects that application's current Turbo Boost state.
